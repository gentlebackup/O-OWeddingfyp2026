Our Story

In a world full of chance encounters, some meetings feel written in the stars. Oluwapelumi and Oluwasegun’s story began with a simple introduction that quickly grew into something far deeper — a connection rooted in shared faith, endless laughter, and a quiet understanding that neither had ever known before.

Through seasons of friendship, prayer, and growing affection, they discovered that love is not only found in grand gestures, but in the everyday moments: the conversations that stretch late into the night, the comfort of presence, and the joy of building dreams side by side.

When Oluwasegun asked Oluwapelumi to spend forever with him, it was the natural next chapter of a story already filled with grace. Now, surrounded by family and friends, they invite you to celebrate the beginning of their new life together — a union grounded in love, faith, and the promise of a beautiful future.
